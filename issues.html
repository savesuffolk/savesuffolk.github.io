What the EAF says lives there

"Black‑capped Chickadee, White‑Breasted Nuthatch, Pine Warbler, Eastern Wood Peewee, Blue Jay, Downy Woodpecker, Whitetail Deer, Eastern Chipmunk, and Eastern Gray Squirrel." The EAF answers "No" to every wetland question, while the site plan's own General Note 3 says wetland and natural‑resource areas "are still being researched." The Town's application form has "Yes" checked for within 500 feet of a stream, drainage channel or wetland.

This is the largest block of vacant land in the entire Sunrise Highway corridor study area (Suffolk County Planning, 2009). Once cleared, it does not come back.
