Our drinking water

Every drop of drinking water in Suffolk County comes from the ground beneath it — one aquifer shared by 1.5 million people. The site sits over it, 19 feet above the water table in fast-draining sand, between two well fields and upstream of Sans Souci Lakes, Brown's River and the Great South Bay. The plan sends runoff from 84 acres of roof and truck court straight into the ground. Watch what changes.
