Meetings & hearings

The zone change is decided at the Islip Town Board's Change of Zone hearings. Planning Board meetings shape the environmental review. County committees control sewer capacity, county roads and any county resolution. Show up. Speak for three minutes. Bring two neighbors.

    Before you go

    Pick a topic

    Speakers who repeat each other get tuned out. Use the 3‑minute statement builder to claim a topic — traffic, water, tax breaks, noise, zoning — and coordinate in the Facebook group.

    Ask a question, not just an objection

    "How will the Town verify Amazon's operator‑supplied truck schedules?" goes on the record and has to be answered. "I'm against it" does not. Every issue card ends with a question.

    Say where you live

    Name your hamlet and street. Officials count constituents. Residents of Sayville, Bohemia, Bayport, Ronkonkoma and Patchogue share the roads, the sewer district and the aquifer — this is a county issue, not a Holbrook issue.
