# What the filing itself says about power, waste and services (read 2026-09-20)

Source: `docs/Full-EAF-2026-07-15.pdf` (pdftotext) and `docs/Traffic-Impact-Study-2026-06-30.pdf` Table 1.

- **Electricity:** "Electrical load is estimated 12.1± MW peak demand." Supplier: "PSEG Long Island, on-site renewable (solar)." (EAF §D.2.k)
- **Grid infrastructure on site:** "Several Long Island Power Authority (LIPA)–owned rights-of-way (ROWs), containing electric transmission towers, traverse the eastern and western portions of the site, with an additional LIPA transmission corridor located along the northern boundary." Beacon Drive access "will require access easements across the LIPA right-of-way." Approvals table: "Access Easement to Cross LIPA-Owned Utility Right-of-way; Electric Connection"; National Grid "Natural Gas Connection (potential)."
- **Upgrades:** "Consultations are being conducted with Suffolk County Water Authority and Suffolk County Department of Public Works to determine if utility upgrades are needed." No statement about PSEG/LIPA capacity.
- **Air/GHG:** stationary sources during operations: "Generator / HVAC systems"; mobile: "Tractor trailers." Air-permit questions (§D.2.g i–iv) left blank with "*See note below": "An air quality and direct and indirect greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions assessment is being prepared." The >10,000 t CO2e/yr checkbox is not answered.
- **Solid waste:** 1,400 t/yr construction; 4,000 t/yr operation; carted off-site; no hazardous waste (§D.2.r–t).
- **Services named:** Sachem Central School District; Suffolk County PD Fifth Precinct; Holbrook Fire Department (fire + EMS); seven parks; Brightview Sayville 1,200 ft SW.
- **Facility types (TIS Table 1):** "1,254,400 SF ARS FC – GEN 14" (cars 143 AM / 767 PM peak-hour trips) and "425,000 SF SSD + Injection" (Flex cars 288 AM / 204 PM). 120 surface stalls "for 'Flex' passenger vehicles being used for 'Same Day Delivery' service." Three shuttle pick-up points for transit; no transit credit taken.
- **Sustainability claims (attachment):** high-SRI concrete in truck courts, white roof, rooftop solar, heat pumps, EV charging, permeable pavement for pedestrian areas — all described as intent, none quantified.

Use: these are the applicant's words; quote them. Open questions for the Town: PSEG/LIPA load letter and who pays for substation/feeder work; generator fuel type, size and run hours; whether the 4,888 Flex trips were modeled on residential streets; GHG total.
